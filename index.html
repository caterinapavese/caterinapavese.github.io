About

Welcome to my website. I am Caterina Pavese, Assistant Professor (tenure track) at the University of Bologna. I am a member of the CESifo network and a research professor at the ifo Institute in Munich.

I obtained my PhD from Ca' Foscari University of Venice in 2020, and was a Visiting Scholar at UCL London in 2019.

My main research interests are in the field of Economics of Education, Migration and Labour Economics.
